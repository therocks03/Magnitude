Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between the three plans? +
Basic is for light guidance with weekly check-ins. Core provides comprehensive support with 2-3 weekly sessions and daily tracking (recommended for most students). Premium offers intensive support with fast response times and daily monitoring for students who need maximum accountability.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan? +
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your mentorship plan at any time based on your needs and preparation intensity.
How quickly will I get responses to my doubts? +
Basic: Within 24 hours. Core: Within 6-8 hours during fixed support hours. Premium: Within 2-4 hours throughout the day.
Who will be my mentor? +
You'll be assigned a dedicated IITian mentor based on your target exam (JEE/NEET) and preparation level. All our mentors have personally cracked these exams and have teaching experience.
Is there a minimum commitment period? +
We recommend a minimum 3-month commitment to see meaningful results, but you can cancel anytime with 1 month notice.
